Who We Are

About Ambedkar Sikhya

An educational and career-guidance platform dedicated to helping students make informed decisions about education, examinations, admissions and career opportunities.

Our Story

Education. Guidance. Opportunity.

Ambedkar Sikhya was created to bridge the information gap that many students face when making critical education and career decisions. We believe every student deserves access to clear, reliable, source-backed guidance — regardless of their background.

Our platform combines home tuition services, career counselling, educational updates, and a growing YouTube channel to serve students from Class 6 through Post-Graduation and beyond.

Our Focus

Education | Guidance | Career | Opportunities

Our Vision

To make quality educational information accessible, understandable and useful for every student.

Our Mission

Educate students, guide careers and connect learners with opportunities.
